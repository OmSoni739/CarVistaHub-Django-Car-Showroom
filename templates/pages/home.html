Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, pricing, and showroom visits.

How do I book a test drive through CarVistaHub? expand_more
Simply find the car you're interested in, click 'Book Test Drive', and select your preferred verified showroom and convenient time slot. Our partner showroom will contact you to confirm and prepare the vehicle.
Are all prices shown on CarVistaHub final on-road prices? expand_more
Prices listed represent standard ex-showroom base prices in INR (₹). Final on-road prices may vary slightly based on state registration fees, road taxes, insurance choices, and dealership accessories.
Can I compare electric cars (EV) with petrol or hybrid cars? expand_more
Yes! Our comparison engine handles cross-category analysis, including battery range to fuel efficiency conversions, torque comparisons, and running cost estimates.
What documents do I need to carry for a test drive? expand_more
You only need a valid Indian Driving License and a government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport). The dealership will verify these before your driving session.
